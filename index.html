Straight answers

Before you upload

Can one photo create an accurate CAD model?

Simple shapes sometimes can, but functional parts normally need multiple views and one known dimension.

Who owns the CAD model?

Ownership and reuse rights are stated in the quote. Choose a package with the STEP file if required.

When do I pay?

The CAD stage is scoped first. Manufacturing is charged after you approve the geometry and production quote.
